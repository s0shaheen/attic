How It Works
Three steps, zero effort
Export your data from TikTok or Instagram, upload it, and Attic does the rest.
1

Export your data

Request your data export from TikTok or Instagram. You'll get a JSON file with all your saved, liked, and favorited content.

2

Attic processes everything

We enrich each video with metadata, extract visual content, and classify it across 8 facets using multi-modal AI. Every piece of content gets deep analysis.

3

Search and ask anything

Chat with your content library. "What restaurants do I have saved?" "Show me workout videos." "Find that recipe from last month." Attic finds it.
